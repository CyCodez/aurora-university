ADMISSIONS

How to apply

Find the information you need to prepare and submit your admission application.

Before you begin

  • Create a student account with a valid email address.
  • Choose a programme from the course catalogue.
  • Prepare your basic contact details and a short personal statement.
  • Review your application before submitting it.

Application requirements

  • Applicants should be at least 16 years old.
  • Full name and valid email address.
  • A selected programme.
  • A short statement explaining your interest in the programme.

Applicants should confirm programme-specific requirements with the admissions office.

1. Create an account

Register with your name, email and phone number.

2. Select a course

Search the course catalogue and check the estimated fee.

3. Submit and track

Complete the application form and follow its status in your dashboard.
